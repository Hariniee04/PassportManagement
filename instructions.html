1. Before Your Appointment

  • Check your appointment date, reporting time, and PSK location carefully.
  • Carry a printed copy of your Appointment Confirmation Slip / Application Reference Number (ARN).
  • Arrive at the Passport Office / PSK at least 15 minutes before your scheduled reporting time.
  • Ensure all details submitted online match your physical identity documents exactly.
  • If any details have changed after online submission, notify the counter officer during document verification.

Important Note: Documents uploaded online during application submission are for reference only. Original documents MUST be physically produced at the PSK.

3. What Happens at the Passport Office

  1. Token Issuance: Show your ARN slip at the entry counter to receive your processing token number.
  2. Document Verification: The Verification Officer will inspect your original documents against your submitted application.
  3. Photograph & Biometric Capture: Live facial photograph and fingerprint scans will be recorded at the counters.
  4. Verification Review: Officer will confirm identity checks and request police verification or forward your case to the Passport Officer.

4. Tatkaal Service Specific Instructions

As your application is submitted under Tatkaal Service, please note:

  • Bring your completed and signed Tatkaal Annexure Undertaking along with original supporting proof.
  • You must produce 3 acceptable original Annexure documents as declared in your application.
  • Police verification for eligible Tatkaal applicants is conducted on a post-passport-issuance basis.
Important Notice

Do not bring digital copies on mobile phones only. You MUST produce physical original documents. Appointment booking does not guarantee automatic passport issuance; final approval is subject to physical verification and administrative clearance.
